# Appendix G: The Gazetteer

> *Ninety-six by seventy-two. One region at a time. One piece of ground one body can cross in a day and get back from.*
> Recorded: the fourth hand, appending

**Hand:** Grieve Mourne, compiling · **Kept:** loose in the second book at leaf eleven, between the bonds and the words

The Vale is ninety-six by seventy-two, one region at a time, which is as much ground as one body can cross in a day and get back. The numbers are what the Wayfarers write on the back of the folded page. The numbers are what the Hold uses when the Hold has to decide which road to walk today. The numbers are not paces. The numbers are not days. The numbers are the day's walk out and the day's walk back, and the day's walk out is the day's walk out and the day's walk back is the day's walk back, and the Hold walks one and walks the other and the Hold is back by dusk.

What this means in the body: you do not go to two places. You go to the mere or you go to the scarp, and whichever one you go to is the one the day is spent on.

This appendix is the gazetteer. The gazetteer is the gazetteer of the Vale and the gazetteer is what the gazetteer is for.

## Ashfall Hold

The settlement. A Hold of twenty-six working people, founded day 1 on a low rise above the fields, with water on two sides of it because Oda Ashfall stood on the rise on day 1 and said she wanted to see the grass move.

Six buildings.

### The Hearth

The hub. The only safe fire. The kitchen, the counting house, the place the board is on the wall. Stone for the lower two thirds, timber above, with the timber blackened at the lintel in a way nobody has done anything about because the blackening is what the fire does. The flags laid by four Carvers on day 31. The board of ash, planed, six feet long, two feet and a hand high, set into the north wall at table height. The grain runs horizontal. The knot is at the lower right.

The Hearth is where the Wake is held. The Wake is no liturgy. The Hold and a cask in the yard, every name spoken once, oldest first. People stay out until light.

### The Memorial

The name-panes. The place the Vigils are held. The Telling is held here, with the panes behind the half circle and the ash pit to the east and the open air behind the open air. The Telling takes about an hour.

The panes are in the eastern range and the western range. The eastern range is for the buried. The western range is for the burned. Oda Ashfall's pane is the oldest pane in the Memorial and the only one with room left on it. Bel Kettle's pane has the K wrong. The pane that says *Grieve Mourne* is the pane of a Teller who was alive when the pane was cut and the pane is glass without pearl and the pearl is for the pyre and the pyre is for the dead.

### The Smithy

Hot end and cold end. The hot end is where the sand meets the pearl and the glass runs at a heat a wood fire can reach. The cold end is where the relics sit on the shelf and the Ashblade hangs on the hook when the Ashblade is not being carried.

The Smithy is where Ketil Brack forged the Ashblade out of a plough coulter and cart iron in two days and was not proud of it. The Smithy is where the Pike was rebound three times with three different relics. The Smithy is where relics too small to bind sit on a shelf and wait for somebody to decide what to do with relics too small to bind.

### The Fields and Workshop

The Fields are the fields. Wrenfield is the near fields and the long grass past the last fence and the place the Hold eats from and the place the Stalkers come out of. The Workshop is the Workshop. The soap-makers are in the Workshop after the Wake, when the soap-makers are not soap-makers and the soap-makers are pearl-makers.

The rota is the rota. The rota is what the rota is for. The rota is the rota of a Hold of twenty-six working people and the rota is on the wall at the Hearth and the rota is what the rota is for.

### The Palisade

Stone for two thirds of its length. Timber on the north-east arc above the mere road, because Asha Vane was conscripted on day 95 and the four Carvers became three and the wall became timber where it should have been stone. Relic set at the stone corners. Yeva Holt's Echo came over the timber on day 134 by driving the Ashblade into it twice at head height and using the blade as a step. Nothing else in the Vale has ever done that.

The ring by the mere gate. Six paces of the Hold's own yard, greener than the rest, where Yeva Holt's Echo went down. Nobody mows it and nobody has said why not.

The palisade is what the palisade is for.

## The Vale around the Hold

### Wrenfield

The near fields. The grass. The last fence. The long grass past the last fence, where the Stalkers come out of.

Wrenfield went Gone on day 152 and was pushed back from Gone on day 163 in an eleven-day piece of work that cost every relic on the shelf except one and the lime from the scarp and twenty-two people.

The Wrenfield Monument is in the middle of Wrenfield at eleven paces of Quick. The Monument is the only Quick in Wrenfield. The Monument is what Wrenfield's Monument is and the Monument is the relic and the relic is somebody.

### The Grey Ash

North. Under the Mourne. Fall-ash and drawn-ash mixed together past the telling apart. Ankle deep where the wind banks it. Trees standing dead upright in it.

The Yielding walk here. Seven people from Ashfall Hold have walked here to sit down. Sila Grey was one of them. The Ash Orchard is at the north end of the Grey Ash. The Ash Orchard is where the Yielding has been being yielded for as long as the Vale has been the Vale.

Colm Grey died here. Nix Ember died here. Wren Ashfall died here. The deaths are in the grass the grass is in.

### Stonewake Scarp

East of the Hold. The quarry. The lime-kilns below the quarry with the white dust on everything within a hundred feet. The barrows above the quarry, in a line that is too straight to be accident, with the openings all facing south.

Eight of the eleven barrows have been broken into. Three wielders have died in the same fifty feet of the scarp. The lime is still brought down from the scarp because the smithy needs the lime and the smithy is what the smithy is for.

The Gravewardens are on the scarp. Halde is on the scarp and has not been put down. Maren did not draw on Halde on day 53 and the Cull has not forgiven it and the Cull is not going to forgive it.

### Kettlemere

South-east of the scarp. The lake. The mere. The Kettle feeds it. Under the lake is Low Kettle, a village drowned before the Hold.

Bel Kettle was carried up and laid on the rise before the water closed. She was eleven. Her Echo starved at the rim for forty years and was reclaimed by Maren Verren on day 38 with the Widow's Maul. The knife and the left shoe came back up out of the lake with the Maul.

The Yielding drank from the lake. The Wayfarers cut the Flask with mere-water and the Flask does not burn and the Flask does not give back the hold.

The Vale has no verb for water. Grieve Mourne has tried twice. The water did not take the ash.

### Brackwood

South. Trees. The charcoal hearths. The burners' camps. The quarantine huts, put up in a hurry on day 41 and still standing.

Nine charcoal burners came back from a Greying stand with grey in the seams of their hands and something wrong with their sleep. The sickness was drawn-ash in the lungs. The Cull argued to cull. Maren walked into the quarantine and sat down in it. The nine were kept. Four died anyway. Five survived. Colm Brack is the one who can no longer hold a cup for a full minute. He is alive in the Hold today.

The second Monument is in Brackwood with the relic from Maren's Echo. The third is at the second stand. The Brackwood ground was held by the relics and the Brackwood ground went back to Greying when nobody maintained it.

### The Vane Road

West of Wrenfield. The road runs south to the Vane Hold and the Vane Hold wall. The wall is four hundred paces of dressed stone, four hundred paces of course after course of stone set on purpose by a Hold that was trying. The wall has been there longer than the Hold has been on the rise.

The Vane Hold gate is barred with two arms' worth of squared oak in iron brackets set into the stone, made for the purpose, by a smith, on a day when the gate was still being opened. The timber is scored. The scoring was done from the inside.

Inside the wall: a Hearth with a board legible. Memory +9, Mercy -23, Communion -44, Dominion +51. *Drifting toward: The Long Hold.* One hundred and sixteen Husks caught in the chores they were doing when the Loosing took them.

The Vane Hold wall is what the Wayfarers call work of giants, and they do not mean tall men.

## Tile substances, canon

The ground of the Vale is eight substances. The substances are the substances and the substances are what the substances are.

**Water.** Kettlemere. The mere. The lake. The substance of the lake is the substance the Yielding drinks and the substance the Wayfarers cut the Flask with. Echoes do not come out of water. Echoes do not feed in water. Bel Kettle sat at the second growth for forty years because water starves an Echo.

**Sand.** Stonewake Scarp. The quarry. The substance of the scarp. The Gravewardens come out of the scarp because the scarp is the Gravewardens' ground and the Gravewardens are what the scarp is for.

**Grass.** Wrenfield. The fields. The substance of the fields. The Stalkers come out of the grass because the grass is the Stalkers' ground and the grass is what the Stalkers are for.

**Tall grass.** Past the last fence. The substance of the long grass. The Stalkers come out of the tall grass because the tall grass is the tall grass and the tall grass is the Stalkers' ground. Rin Ashfall died in the tall grass. Maren Verren died in the tall grass at the foot of a quarry face that was not the tall grass.

**Ash.** The Grey Ash. The substance of the ash. The Ash Husks come out of the ash. The Yielding walks into the ash. The trees stand dead upright in the ash. The ash is the ash and the ash is what the ash is for.

**Rock.** Stonewake Scarp above the quarry. The kilns. The substance of the scarp's upper face.

**Tree.** Brackwood. The substance of the trees. Nothing comes out of the trees. The trees are what the trees are for.

**Wall.** The Vane Road. The substance of the wall. Nothing comes out of the wall. The wall is what the wall is for.

## Decay states

The Vale has five decay states. The states are what the ground moves through when the Echoes feed on it. The states are named Quick and Turning and Greying and Ashen and Gone. The states are the states and the states are what the states are for.

| Value | Name | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Quick** | green, wet, loud with insects |
| 1 | **Turning** | grass going pale at the root, birds thinner |
| 2 | **Greying** | ash in the seams of everything, colour draining |
| 3 | **Ashen** | drawn-ash over the soil, trees standing dead upright |
| 4 | **Gone** | no sound, no growth, ash to the ankle, routes closed |

**Quick** is the state the ground starts at. The Hold's near fields are Quick because the Hold has pushed back. Brackwood's second stand is Quick because of two Monuments. The eleven paces around the Wrenfield Monument are Quick in the middle of Gone because the relic under the stone is doing the work the relic was bound to do.

**Turning** is the state the ground goes to when the Echoes start feeding. The grass pales at the root. The birds thin out. The Hold notices. The Hold has noticed three times and the Hold has done the arithmetic three times.

**Greying** is the state the ground goes to when the Echoes have been feeding for a season. The ash is in the seams of everything. The colour drains. The Cinder Stalkers come out of Greying ground because the Stalkers feed on Greying and the Greying is what the Stalkers are for.

**Ashen** is the state the ground goes to when the Echoes have been feeding for two seasons. Drawn-ash over the soil. The trees are standing dead upright. The Ash Husks come out of Ashen ground because the Husks feed on Ashen and the Ashen is what the Husks are for.

**Gone** is the state the ground goes to when the Echoes have been feeding for three or more seasons. No sound. No growth. Ash to the ankle. The routes are closed. The Gravewardens come out of Gone ground because the Gravewardens are what Gone is for.

The Echo's feeding moves the ground from Quick to Turning in one growth. The Echo's feeding moves the ground from Turning to Greying in two more. The Echo's feeding moves the ground from Greying to Ashen in two more. The Echo's feeding moves the ground from Ashen to Gone in two more. The Echo's feeding moves the ground from Gone to beyond in two more, and beyond is what the Yielding walks.

## Encounters come out of what

The Vale has eight substances. Encounters come out of two of them.

Cinder Stalkers come out of tall grass. Cinder Stalkers come out of ash. Cinder Stalkers come out of Greying ground and Ashen ground and Gone ground.

Ash Husks come out of ash. Ash Husks come out of Greying and Ashen and Gone.

Gravewardens come out of rock. Gravewardens come out of stone, packed in plate, with relics in the plate.

Echoes come out of wherever the Echo died. Echoes do not come out of anything because Echoes are the grip of the dead walking.

The Hold has Stalkers in the tall grass. The Hold has Husks in the ash. The Hold has Gravewardens in the rock. The Hold has no encounters in the trees. The trees are what the trees are.

The Foragers explain why.

A Stalker is a Vale animal that learned hold is food. The animal was changed by drawn-ash and the changing is what the Stalker is. The Stalkers come out of where the drawn-ash is and the drawn-ash is in the tall grass and in the ash.

A Husk is a villager whose hold was never taken up. The Husk walks the last chore the Husk walked. The Husk comes out of where the drawn-ash has been and the drawn-ash has been in the ash and the ash is where the husks are.

A Gravewarden is what a Warden becomes when the Warden has bound relics into the Warden's own plate. The Gravewarden is old. The Gravewarden has been standing for as long as the scarp has been a scarp. The Gravewarden comes out of the scarp because the Gravewardens are in the scarp and the scarp is what the Gravewardens are for.

This is the Foragers' craft. The Foragers explain why. The Foragers are not always right but the Foragers are always the people who walk the ground and the walking is what the Foragers are for.

## The fall-ash and drawn-ash test

A Forager can tell fall-ash from drawn-ash. The Foragers in the Hold can tell. The test is the test and the test is what the Foragers do and the Foragers do it because the Foragers are the Foragers.

The test, by hand. Fall-ash crumbles between the fingers and the crumbling is dry. Drawn-ash stays together between the fingers and the staying is wet. The dry is the fall and the wet is the draw and the draw is what the drawn-ash has been through.

The test, by what grows. Fall-ash grows barley the first season after the ashfall and the barley is the best barley anyone has seen. Drawn-ash grows nothing. Drawn-ash is the ash the ground has been fed on. Drawn-ash is the ash the Echo has been fed with.

The test, by what it takes to fix. Fall-ash locks in the fifth season and the locking is what the bone-and-lye is for. Drawn-ash does not lock because drawn-ash is not ground that grows and the ground that does not grow is not ground the bone-and-lye can fix.

This is the test. The test is on the hands and the hands are the hands and the hands are the Foragers' hands and the Foragers' hands are what the Foragers' hands are for.

## The lime-kilns and the pearl chain

The lime-kilns. Below the quarry. Three kilns, fired all summer, with wood cut and carted to burn under them and somebody sitting at the end of every week counting what came out. The white dust on everything within a hundred feet. The dust on the boots of the kiln-shifts. The dust in the seams of the Foragers' hands.

The lime goes to the Smithy. The lime goes to the beds of the wall. The lime is what the scarp gives and the scarp is what the lime is for.

The pearl chain.

Pyre-ash steeped in water for three days. Drawn off clear. The water is lye. The lye is what soap is made of and what pot-ash is made of. The pot-ash is boiled dry in the iron pots. The pot-ash is refined again in the smaller pot. The refined pot-ash is pearl.

Pearl into the sand at the Smithy's hot end. The pearl is what makes the glass run at a heat a wood fire can reach. Glass is sand melted. Sand needs a heat a wood fire cannot reach. Sand with pearl melts at a heat a wood fire can reach. The glass is what the panes are. The panes are what the Memorial is for.

This is the pearl chain. The chain is: pyre-ash, lye, pot-ash, pearl, glass, pane. The chain is the chain and the chain is what the chain is for.

The chain has no end and the chain has no beginning. The chain has pyre-ash at one end and a pane at the other and a body in between.

## The ninety-six by seventy-two

The number is not paces. The number is not days. The number is what the Wayfarers write on the back of the folded page and the number is what the Hold uses when the Hold has to decide which road to walk today.

The number is the day's walk out and the day's walk back. The day's walk out is from the Hold's gate to the edge of the place the Hold can walk in a day. The day's walk back is from the edge back to the Hold's gate. The day's walk out and the day's walk back are the same number of paces because the day's walk back is the day's walk out turned around.

The number is what the Hold can defend. The number is what the Hold can leave and the Hold can come back to. The number is the day's worth of ground and the day's worth of ground is what the wielder walks.

I have asked four Wayfarers and been given three answers and a shrug. I have not written the three answers down because the three answers did not agree and the third answer was a shrug and the shrug is not an answer and the shrug is the shrug.

I have written down what the number means in the body. The number means: one place at a time. One region at a time. One piece of ground one body can cross in a day and get back from.

This is the number. The number is the number and the number is what the number is for.
