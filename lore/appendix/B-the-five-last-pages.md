# Appendix B: The Five Last Pages, Resolved

> *The board does not average. The order of questions decides.*
> Recorded: the fourth hand, appending, with worked examples

**Hand:** Grieve Mourne, compiling · **Kept:** loose in the second book at leaf five, between the roll and the first entry

This appendix is the appendix the Teller keeps when the Teller wants to know what the board is doing. The Teller does not keep this appendix for the ending. The Teller keeps this appendix for the question.

The question is: what would the board say about my Hold if my Hold had the same numbers but a different set of Echoes reclaimed.

The question has five answers. The five answers are the five endings. The five endings are checked in this order, first match wins. A positive requirement is a floor. A negative requirement is a ceiling.

This appendix is the working note. The working note is on the page because the page is the page and the working note is the working note and the working note is what the working note is for.

## The board's procedure, in order

1. **The Wake.** Memory at or above forty-five, Communion at or above forty, every Echo reclaimed, the lineage not empty.
2. **The Long Hold.** Dominion at or above fifty, Mercy at or below minus twenty.
3. **Ash Inheritance.** Memory at or below minus forty.
4. **The Open Hearth.** Communion at or above fifty-five, Mercy at or above thirty-five.
5. **The Quiet Field.** Always.

The board checks the first question. If the first question matches, the board reads the first ending. The board does not check the second. The board does not check the third. The board does not check the fourth. The board does not check the fifth.

If the first question does not match, the board checks the second. If the second matches, the board reads the second ending. The board does not check the third, the fourth, the fifth.

And so on. The order is the order. The order is what the order is.

The first question is checked first. The first question is the Wake. The Wake is checked first because the Wake is the only ending that requires every Echo reclaimed, and every Echo reclaimed is the hardest thing a Hold can do, and the hardest thing is checked first because the hardest thing is the thing that matters most.

The fourth question is checked fourth. The fourth question is the Open Hearth. The Open Hearth is checked fourth because the Open Hearth is the ending that requires Communion and Mercy and does not require every Echo reclaimed, and the Open Hearth is the ending a Hold drifts to when the Hold has been generous and the Hold has been merciful and the Hold has not done the hardest thing.

This is the order. The order is the order. The order is the thing the order is.

## Why the order matters: Ashfall and Holt Vale

On the one hundred and sixteenth day of Ashfall Hold, Memory clamped at one hundred, Communion stood at eighty, Mercy at seventy-three, Dominion at five. Every Echo had been reclaimed. The lineage was not empty. The Wake gate was at Memory forty-five and Communion forty and every Echo reclaimed and not empty lineage. Memory was at one hundred. Communion was at eighty. Every Echo was reclaimed. The lineage was not empty.

The Wake was at the gate. The Wake locked. The board read The Wake.

On the one hundred and sixteenth day of Holt Vale, the numbers were the same, or close enough. Memory clamped at one hundred. Communion stood at eighty. Mercy at seventy-three. Dominion at five. Foreign Echoes were at the palisade and the foreign Echoes were not on the board as Echoes and the Wake was not at the gate because the Wake was not at the gate. The Open Hearth was at the gate. The Open Hearth was at the gate because Communion was at eighty and Mercy was at seventy-three and the Open Hearth gate was at fifty-five and thirty-five.

The Open Hearth was at the gate. The board read The Open Hearth.

Ashfall Hold and Holt Vale had the same numbers. Ashfall Hold got The Wake. Holt Vale got The Open Hearth. The board's order of questions decided which one got which.

This is the case I find intolerable and that I have recorded.

## Vane Hold, day 88

Vane Hold's board, on day 88 of their Chronicle, read:

Memory plus nine. Mercy minus twenty-three. Communion minus forty-four. Dominion plus fifty-one. *Drifting toward: The Long Hold.*

The Long Hold was at the gate. The Long Hold gate was Dominion at or above fifty and Mercy at or below minus twenty. Dominion was at fifty-one. Mercy was at minus twenty-three.

The Long Hold was at the gate. The board read The Long Hold. The board did not check The Wake. The board did not check Ash Inheritance. The board did not check The Open Hearth. The board checked The Long Hold and The Long Hold was at the gate and The Long Hold locked.

Vane Hold died at The Long Hold. Vane Hold's wall is still standing. Vane Hold's gate is still barred. Vane Hold's Husks are still at their chores. Vane Hold's Echoes are still roaming in four valleys.

This is the case I find correct and that I have recorded.

## Holt Vale, day 246

Holt Vale's board, on day 246 of their Chronicle, read:

Memory clamped at one hundred. Mercy at ninety. Communion at one hundred. Dominion at one. *Drifting toward: The Open Hearth.*

The Open Hearth was at the gate. The Open Hearth gate was Communion at or above fifty-five and Mercy at or above thirty-five. Communion was at one hundred. Mercy was at ninety.

The Wake gate was also at the gate. The Wake gate was Memory at or above forty-five and Communion at or above forty and every Echo reclaimed. Memory was at one hundred. Communion was at one hundred. The Wake's first three conditions were met.

The Wake's fourth condition was not met. The Echoes were not reclaimed. Foreign Echoes were at the palisade and the foreign Echoes were at the fifth growth and the foreign Echoes were not Holt Vale's Echoes.

The board checks The Wake first. The Wake's first three conditions matched. The Wake's fourth condition did not. The Wake was not at the gate. The board did not lock The Wake.

The board checks The Long Hold second. Dominion was at one. The Long Hold gate was Dominion at or above fifty. The Long Hold was not at the gate.

The board checks Ash Inheritance third. Memory was at one hundred. Ash Inheritance gate was Memory at or below minus forty. Ash Inheritance was not at the gate.

The board checks The Open Hearth fourth. Communion was at one hundred and Mercy was at ninety and the Open Hearth gate was Communion at or above fifty-five and Mercy at or above thirty-five. The Open Hearth was at the gate. The board read The Open Hearth. The board locked The Open Hearth.

Holt Vale died at The Open Hearth. Holt Vale's Memorial has a third strangers. Holt Vale's road has traffic on it.

This is the case that has the road on it.

## What the board cannot know

The board is a piece of wood with numbers on it. The board does not know what the numbers mean. The board does not know whose Echo is at the palisade. The board does not know which valley the Echo came from. The board does not know whose Teller stopped keeping the Chronicle and whose Chronicle the Echo came out of.

The board knows four numbers and a fifth line. The board reads what the four numbers and the fifth line say. The board does not read what the four numbers and the fifth line do not say.

Two cases of what the board cannot know.

The first case is Kettlemere. Kettlemere is a lake. Kettlemere has a village under it. The village's dead are in the Holding at a depth no Teller can survey. The board does not count what no Teller can survey. The board has never counted Kettlemere's dead. The board has never known Kettlemere's dead are there. The Wake was declared over a valley with a village still unkept under the water. Grieve Mourne knows. The board does not.

The second case is the illegible tier four from chapter forty-two. The tier four came over the wall on day 196 and the tier four's name was on a belt in a hand the Hold could not read and the hand was not Bran Holt's hand and the hand was not Asha Vane's hand and the hand was not a hand the Hold knew. The board entered the tier four as `wielder_died` for Orrin Dunn. The board did not enter the tier four's owner. The owner was on nobody's board. The owner was a person whose Teller stopped.

The board is honest about what a Teller entered. The board is not honest about what a Teller did not enter. The board's honesty is the board's limitation. The board's limitation is what the board cannot know.

This is the appendix. The appendix is what the appendix is for. The appendix is for the Teller who wants to know what the board is doing and the board is doing what the board is doing and the board is doing what the board can do and the board cannot know what the board cannot know.

## Working rule, in clerical voice

If your Hold has Memory above forty-five and Communion above forty, look at your Echoes. If every Echo is reclaimed and your lineage is not empty, the Wake is at the gate.

If the Wake is not at the gate because of the Echoes, look at your Dominion. If Dominion is above fifty and your Mercy is below minus twenty, the Long Hold is at the gate.

If the Long Hold is not at the gate, look at your Memory. If Memory is below minus forty, Ash Inheritance is at the gate.

If Ash Inheritance is not at the gate, look at your Communion and your Mercy. If Communion is above fifty-five and Mercy is above thirty-five, the Open Hearth is at the gate.

If none of these are at the gate, the board reads The Quiet Field. The Quiet Field is what the board reads when nothing else matches. The Quiet Field is what most settlements are most of the time. The Quiet Field is the commonest ending in the world.

The board does not average. The board does not settle. The board reads what the board reads.

The Teller records what was said.
