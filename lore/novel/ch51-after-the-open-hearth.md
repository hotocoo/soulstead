# Chapter 51: After the Open Hearth

**Hand:** Tove Rowan, then four Tellers, then no attribution at all · **Place:** eleven valleys

I am Tove Rowan. I am fifty-eight. I have not held a blade in nine years. I am writing this in Holt Vale at the Memorial, on the eastern range, at the pane that says *Nix Ember*. The pane is glass and the glass has Nix Ember's pearl and the pearl is in the glass and the glass is the glass.

I am writing this because the Chronicle of the eleven valleys has reached me and the Chronicle is eleven boards and the boards are stained with the stains of eleven valleys and the stains are not water and the stains are not ash and the stains are what the stains are.

The Chronicle is the Chronicle of the Open Hearth. The Chronicle is the Chronicle of the eleven valleys that have decided to bury strangers. The Chronicle is the Chronicle of the road that has traffic on it because the road has traffic on it.

·  ·  ·

What crossing between holds looks like, at eleven valleys.

Route-marks at every crossing. The route-marks are six characters cut into a post, and the post is at the corner of a Hold, and the corner is the corner where the road turns. The route-marks are fresh. The route-marks are re-cut every season by a Carver or a Teller or a person whose name is not on the route-mark.

Tellers who have never met copying each other's entries within a season. The copying is the copying and the copying is what the copying is. The Tellers are in eleven valleys and the Tellers do not know each other's names and the Tellers are copying each other's entries and the entries are the entries and the entries are what the entries are.

A shared drift that no single hold can move alone. The drift is the drift of eleven boards and the eleven boards are the eleven boards and the eleven boards are drifting toward the same ending because the eleven boards have the same numbers and the numbers are the numbers the numbers are.

The first argument in history conducted between two boards. The argument was about whether to shave the panes of a third hold whose Tellers had stopped keeping the Chronicle. The argument was held at a crossing post by a Teller from each of two valleys and the Teller from each of two valleys argued for an hour and the argument was settled by a third Teller who arrived on the road and who was not invited and who said that the panes were not theirs to shave.

The mechanism has stopped being a mechanism. The mechanism has become a culture. The mechanism is what people do and the people are the people and the people do what the people do because the people do what the people do.

·  ·  ·

The async mechanism, made lore.

An Echo is written onto a folded page. The folded page is carried out of the valley. The folded page arrives in a valley whose people never met the Echo's owner. The folded page is read by a Teller who did not write the Echo. The Teller enters the Echo on the board. The Echo is buried in the second valley. The burial is an entry on the second valley's board. The entry walks home in somebody else's hand. The somebody else is a Wayfarer. The Wayfarer carries the entry to the first valley. The first valley reads the entry. The first valley's Teller enters the entry on the first valley's board.

The Echo has come home. The Echo has come home a season after the Echo was carried out. The Echo has come home in somebody else's hand and the somebody else's hand is a Wayfarer's hand and the Wayfarer is a person who walks between valleys and the person is what the person is.

This is the async mechanism. The async mechanism is what crossing between holds is. The async mechanism is the line on the board in the hand on the leaf in the bundle under the arm on the road at the post at the gate at the table in the next valley's Chronicle.

The async mechanism is what makes the Open Hearth reachable. The async mechanism is what crosses between holds. The async mechanism is what the Vale's network is built on. The async mechanism is what the Open Hearth is for.

·  ·  ·

The cost.

I am going to write the cost because the cost is the cost and the cost is what the Open Hearth is paid for.

A cruelty done in one valley now moves the numbers in four valleys. The cruelty is done by a wielder of one Hold and the cruelty moves the board of four Holds because the four Holds have shared numbers and the shared numbers are the shared numbers and the shared numbers move when one moves.

The first hold to work this out on purpose did the cruelty on purpose. The cruelty was `ally_abandoned`. The cruelty was done by a wielder whose name is on a leaf I have, and the wielder was a wielder of a Hold whose name is on the leaf I have. The wielder abandoned an ally in a field and the ally died and the ally's Echo was left and the Echo went around.

The Echo went around because the Echo went around. The Echo fed. The Echo tiered. The Echo's feeding moved the numbers in the four Holds the Echo's drift had crossed. The numbers moved by `region_lost` and `echo_raided` and the numbers moved on four boards and the four boards are the four boards and the four boards are what the four boards are.

The Hold that did the cruelty is not a villain. The Hold that did the cruelty is a Hold of twenty-six people whose wielder made a decision in a field and the decision was a decision and the decision was not the Hold's decision and the decision was the wielder's decision and the wielder is not a villain and the wielder is a person who made a decision in a field.

The Hold that did the cruelty has not been stopped. The Hold that did the cruelty is on the road and the road has traffic on it and the Hold that did the cruelty is one of the four Holds and the four Holds are the four Holds and the cruelty is in the books of the four Holds and the books are not going to scrape the cruelty out.

The cruelty is the cost. The cost is the cost of the Open Hearth. The cost is what the Open Hearth is paid for. The cost is what happens when strangers are buried beside your own and the strangers' Echoes feed on your ground and the feeding moves your board.
