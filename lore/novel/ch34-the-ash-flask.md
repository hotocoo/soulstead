# Chapter 34: The Ash Flask

> *Pyre-ash steeped, settled three days, drawn off clear. Cut with hearth-water until it will not strip a throat.*
> Recorded: the Hearth, with a Healer on one side and the standing ration on the other

**Hand:** Bran Holt, with a Healer's own note · **Days:** 77 to 79 · **Place:** the Hearth, Wrenfield

Three is not caution.

The word *caution* has never been used at the Hearth about the Flask. Caution is what a person says when a person does not know how much is too much. The Hearth knows how much is too much. The Hearth knows how much is too much because the Hearth has lost people to too much, and the losing is on the Healer's note that is pinned to the lintel above the Flask shelf, and the note has been there since the twenty-fourth day of the Hold and the note is not going to be taken down.
