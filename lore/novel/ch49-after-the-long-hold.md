# Chapter 49: After the Long Hold

> *Work of giants. Nobody now living could build it. Nobody now living knows why they would.*
> Recorded: a hold two generations later, copied by nobody

**Hand:** a hold two generations later, copied by nobody · **Place:** the Vane Road, Ashfall's ruin

We came down the Vane Road on the morning the road was open.

The road was open because the road was open and the road has been open since we could not remember and the open road is the road we have walked to and from the road we walk to walk the open road. The road is open because the gate is open and the gate is open because the gate is not there and the gate was a gate and the gate is not there and the gate is not there because the timber rotted and the iron brackets are in a heap and the heap is at the foot of the wall.

The wall is across the road at the head of the valley south of ours. The wall is four hundred paces of level course and the courses are dressed and the joints are close and the lime in the beds is still gripping and the wall is older than the Hold is older than we are.

The wall has a beam failed inward at the north-east corner. The beam is timber and the beam is broken and the beam has been broken for as long as we have been told and the break is the only way in. The break is a doorway now. The doorway is what the doorway was.

We went in. We went in because we came down the road and the road was open and the gate was not there and the break was the doorway and the doorway was the way in.

We went in to see who had been there.

·  ·  ·

Inside the wall.

The wall encloses a square. The square is a hold that was a hold and the hold was a hold that has been a hold for longer than we can tell. The hold has a Hearth. The Hearth has a board.

The board is on the north wall. The board is planed. The board has four numbers cut into it and one line under the four numbers and the line is what the line is and the line is the line that says what the board is drifting toward.

The four numbers are cut. The line is cut. The numbers and the line are legible. We read them.

Memory was nine. Mercy was minus twenty-three. Communion was minus forty-four. Dominion was fifty-one. The line said *Drifting toward* and the drift said *The Long Hold*.

We read them. We have read them since. We are going to keep reading them.

The four numbers are the largest single set of Dominion numbers we have seen on any board in any valley. Memory at nine is the smallest Memory we have ever seen on a board that still has a name on it. The numbers are correct. The numbers were cut by a hand we do not know and the hand was the hand that was here and the hand was the hand that left.

The line under the numbers is a line we have read in three other valleys. The line is at the bottom of every board in every valley. The line says what the board is drifting toward. Nobody knows who put it there. The line was on this board. The line is on every board.

·  ·  ·

The Husk count.

There are one hundred and sixteen Husks inside the wall. They were doing chores when we came in. The Husks were caught in the chores they had been doing when the Loosing took them and the Loosing took them and the chores were the chores the chores were.

Eleven at the looms. Six at the byres. Two in the lane between byre and workshop three feet apart facing each other. Four in the doorway of a smithy with hammers in their hands and the hammers in their hands are hammers they had been holding when the Loosing took them. One at a well handle with her hand on the handle and the handle in the position the handle is in when the handle is being turned. One at a table with both hands flat on a bench worn to its shape. Eleven on the benches in the stone Hearth, with a cask on the Hearth, and the cask is the cask and the Hearth is the Hearth and the benches are the benches.

I counted twice. The count is one hundred and sixteen. The Husks are doing chores and the chores are the chores the Husks were doing when the Loosing took them and the Loosing took them.

The Husks did not move when we came in. The Husks are not moving. The Husks have been doing their chores for as long as the wall has been the wall and the wall has been the wall for as long as the wall has been the wall.

·  ·  ·

We read the board. We read the count. We did not take anything. We did not move anything. We set the timber back against the break and we walked back up the Vane Road.

We did not take anything because the wall is not ours. The wall was built by a hold that was trying and the hold was trying and the hold is not ours. The Husks are not ours. The board is not ours. The board's numbers are the numbers of a Hold that was trying and the trying is not ours.

The work of giants is what the Wayfarers say. We are not Wayfarers and we did not say it. We did not say anything. We walked back up the road and we did not say anything.

·  ·  ·

What we wrote down when we got back to our Hold.

The wall is stone. The wall is dressed. The wall has lime in the beds. The wall has a beam failed inward at the north-east corner. The wall has a Husk count of one hundred and sixteen. The wall has a board with four numbers and a line and the line says *The Long Hold* and the numbers say *Memory nine, Mercy minus twenty-three, Communion minus forty-four, Dominion fifty-one*.

The wall is older than we are. The wall was built by a hold that was trying. The hold was right about the wall. The hold was wrong about everything else.

The wall was right because the wall held. The wall held because nobody went out. Nobody went out because nobody was outside. Nobody was outside because the gate was barred. The gate was barred because nobody went out.

The gate was barred because nobody went out. Nobody went out because the gate was barred.

This is the circle. This is the circle the wall built. This is the circle that holds a hold together when nothing else holds a hold together. The circle is correct. The circle did not save them.

They were correct. They were finished.

·  ·  ·

The rule the Teller wrote.

The Teller of our Hold wrote the rule on the first page of our Chronicle, in the Teller's hand, dated the day we came back from the wall.

*I record what was said. I do not settle it.*

The rule is on the page. The rule is the rule. The rule is what the rule is. The rule is older than the wall and the wall is older than we are and the rule is the rule the rule is.

The Teller had not read the rule anywhere. The Teller had not talked to anyone who had read the rule anywhere. The Teller wrote the rule because the rule is the rule and the rule is the rule the Teller was going to write and the rule was the rule.

The Teller wrote it because the rule is what the rule is. The Teller did not write it from anywhere. The Teller wrote it from the Teller.

We have copied the rule into seven Chronicles we have sent to seven other valleys. The seven Chronicles have copied the rule into their own Chronicles. The rule is in eleven Chronicles in eleven valleys and the rule is the rule and the rule is the same rule in all of them.

I am writing this because I am the second Teller of our Hold and I am writing the wall and the wall is the wall and the wall is what the wall is. I am writing what I have written and I am writing that I do not settle it.

The Long Hold is what the Long Hold is. The Long Hold is not an ending. The Long Hold is a phase in a cycle. The cycle is the only thing in the Vale that never decays.

The cycle is the wall being built. The cycle is the wall holding. The cycle is the gate being barred. The cycle is the gate rotted. The cycle is the road being open. The cycle is the road being walked. The cycle is the new wall. The cycle is the new gate. The cycle is the new bar. The cycle is the new rot. The cycle is the new open.

The cycle is the only thing in the Vale that never decays because the cycle is the Vale and the Vale does not decay and the Vale is the cycle.
