# Chapter 46: The Open Hearth

> *Strangers' Echoes were buried beside your own. Other settlements send travellers now. The valley is bigger than one lineage.*
> Recorded: the fourth hand, day two hundred and forty-six of Holt Vale, with a leaf from Tove Rowan

**Hand:** Tove Rowan, Teller and wielder of Holt Vale · **Days:** their day 160 to their day 246 · **Place:** Holt Vale

I am Tove Rowan. I am forty-one. I take up the Ashblade of Holt Vale on our day one hundred and sixty, and the Ashblade of Holt Vale is not the Ashblade of Ashfall Hold, and the taking up is not the taking up of Ashfall Hold, and the taking up is the taking up of Holt Vale.

The Ashblade of Holt Vale was forged at the smithy of a settlement that was on the ridge before the ridge had a settlement, and the Ashblade of Holt Vale has a curve in it that the smith of the Ashblade of Holt Vale did not put there, and the curve is a piece of the iron the smith of the Ashblade of Holt Vale did not work out, and the curve is what the Ashblade of Holt Vale remembers, and the remembering is what the Ashblade of Holt Vale is for.

I am not writing this in the way I have heard Ashfall's Teller write. I am writing this in the way I write, which is the way of a Teller who keeps loose leaves in a chest and has decided that the chest is the chest and the chest is what the chronicle is for and the chronicle is what we are for. I have read Ashfall's Chronicle. I have Ashfall's Chronicle in seven leaves that were carried west on the one hundred and eighty-ninth day by a Wayfarer whose name is not on the leaf. The seven leaves are the seven leaves and the seven leaves are what I am writing from.

I am going to write three things that differ from Ashfall's account of the one hundred and eightieth day of their Chronicle, and the three things are at the end of this chapter.

·  ·  ·

What crosses between holds is entries.

I am going to spend the first half of this chapter on what crosses between holds, because what crosses between holds is how Holt Vale got where Holt Vale is, and where Holt Vale is, is The Open Hearth.

The Open Hearth is the ending that requires Communion at or above fifty-five and Mercy at or above thirty-five. The Open Hearth is the ending that does not require every Echo reclaimed. The Open Hearth is the ending that does not require Memory at the gate. The Open Hearth is the ending the board reads when the Hold has been generous and the Hold has been merciful and the Hold has opened its gate to strangers.

The Open Hearth is what Ashfall Hold did not get. Ashfall Hold reached the same Communion and the same Mercy and Ashfall Hold got The Wake. The Wake is the ending that requires every Echo reclaimed. The Wake is checked before The Open Hearth on the board. The board checked The Wake first and The Wake was at the gate and The Wake locked and the board did not check The Open Hearth.

I am going to spend a paragraph on what this means.

It means that Ashfall Hold and Holt Vale had the same numbers at the same time and the same Communion and the same Mercy and the board read different endings because the board's order of questions decided. The Wake is checked first. The Wake was at the gate. The Wake locked. The Open Hearth was not checked because The Open Hearth was not checked.

The Open Hearth is the ending Ashfall Hold would have got if Ashfall Hold had not got The Wake. The Open Hearth is the ending Holt Vale got because Holt Vale did not have every Echo reclaimed, and Holt Vale could not have every Echo reclaimed, because Holt Vale's line was thinner than Ashfall's line, and Holt Vale's wielder was in the field every day, and the Echoes that came across the ridge into Holt Vale's barley were Echoes Holt Vale could not afford to chase.

·  ·  ·

How Holt Vale got here.

We bury everything that arrives. Every Echo. Every foreign Echo. Every drifted Echo. Every Echo that crosses the ridge and walks into Holt Vale's barley, we bury. We have buried eleven foreign Echoes in eighty days, from four valleys, and the eleven are the eleven and the eleven are the eleven that have come and the eleven are the eleven that we have buried.

The first was Nix Ember. Nix Ember was an Ashfall wielder. Nix Ember came in with the Ashblade of Ashfall and the Ashblade was the Ashblade of Ashfall and we did not have an Ashblade of Ashfall before. The Ashblade of Ashfall was on the Echo when the Echo was put down and the Ashblade of Ashfall was the Echo's Ashblade and the Ashblade of Ashfall was reclaimed by us. The Ashblade of Ashfall is on a hook in our smithy and the hook is the hook the Ashblade of Ashfall has been on since the one hundred and eighty-ninth day of our Chronicle.

The pane was cut on the one hundred and eighty-ninth day. The pane was in Holt Vale. The pane was fluxed with Nix Ember's pearl and Nix Ember's pearl was in Nix Ember's flask. The pane is on the eastern range at the Memorial and the pane is the pane the Hold will show to whoever asks what the Memorial looks like.

The eleven are buried. Eleven panes, eleven names. Eleven names from eleven Echoes from four valleys. The eleven panes are a third of our Memorial by pane count. The Memorial is a third strangers. The third is what the third is.

·  ·  ·

The cost.

Our own lineage is thinner. Our own Echoes are not all reclaimed because we could not afford to reclaim them. Our wielders have died and we have let their Echoes stand because the Echoes were not at the palisade and the Echoes were not feeding and the Echoes were not what the Echoes would have been if we had gone out and fetched them.

Our Legacies are fewer. The Burials we did not hold are the Burials whose Legacy we do not have. The Pyres we did not hold are the Pyres whose Legacy we do not have. The Tellings we did not hold are the Tellings whose Legacy we do not have. The Legacies we have are the Legacies the strangers gave us by dying in our field, and the strangers' Legacies are the strangers' and not ours.

Our wielders are weaker than Ashfall's. Our wielders carry the build the wielder was born with, plus the stranger's Memory, plus the stranger's Mercy. Our wielders do not have the seven Legacies Ashfall has from the Burials and the Pyres and the Tellings of nine of their own. Our wielders are weaker in the body and stronger in the Communion and the Communion is the trade the Communion is.

We have been raided twice. Twice in eighty days. The raids were Echoes we did not chase. The Echoes were at our gate and we put them down at the gate and the Echoes were not in the Echoes' fields because the Echoes were not the Echoes we should have been chasing. The Echoes were the Echoes we did not have time to chase.

·  ·  ·

The mechanism.

Route-marks on eleven posts. Six characters cut into each post. The marks are the marks Wayfarers have been cutting for as long as Wayfarers have been Wayfarers, and the marks mean *a hold this way is keeping a board you can write on*, and the board is on the way and the board is the board.

Four Tellers, in four valleys, copying both ways. The copies are leaves. The leaves are in chests. The chests are at the four Holds. The four Holds are Holt Vale and three other Holds whose names are on the leaves.

What crosses is entries. The road has traffic because the road has traffic. The road has traffic because there is a hold at the end of it known to bury strangers. The road has traffic because the road has traffic.

This is the mechanism. This is the Open Hearth. The Open Hearth is a logistics achievement. The Open Hearth is not a feeling.

·  ·  ·

The end of the chapter.

I am going to write what the board read on the two hundred and forty-sixth day of Holt Vale. The board read The Open Hearth. The Open Hearth was at the gates. The gates were at the gates. The board had been reading The Open Hearth for thirty-seven days. The board had been reading The Open Hearth because the Echoes at the palisade were foreign Echoes and the foreign Echoes were not on the board as Echoes and the Echoes at the palisade were not the Echoes at the palisade the Wake was reading.

The board had been reading The Open Hearth for thirty-seven days and the thirty-seven days were the thirty-seven days and the board was right about the thirty-seven days because the board was reading what the board was reading.

·  ·  ·

Three particulars that differ from Ashfall's account of the one hundred and eightieth day.

Ashfall's Teller wrote: *Orrin Dunn knelt down in the grass next to her and put his hands on her ribs.*

I am writing what I wrote on the one hundred and eightieth day of our Chronicle, which is the one hundred and eightieth day of Ashfall's Chronicle. I wrote: I was on my back in the grass and the grass was at the back of my head and Orrin Dunn was at my ribs. I wrote: his hands were cold. I wrote: he did not say anything while he was holding my ribs and I did not say anything while he was holding my ribs and the not-saying was the not-saying of two people who did not know each other.

The third particular is this: he did not give me his flask. I drank my own flask. The flask I drank was my flask and the flask was full and the flask was what the flask was. Ashfall's Teller wrote that I drank his flask. I did not drink his flask. I drank mine. The flask was mine.

I record what I said. I do not settle it.

·  ·  ·

The ending text, from the board, copied exactly:

*Strangers' Echoes were buried beside your own. Other settlements send travellers now. The valley is bigger than one lineage.*

---

Grieve Mourne, compiling. I have this leaf in front of me. I have read it nine times. I find it intolerable and I record it.

Ashfall Hold and Holt Vale had the same numbers at the same time. Ashfall Hold had every Echo reclaimed. Holt Vale did not. Ashfall Hold got The Wake. Holt Vale got The Open Hearth.

The board's order of questions decided this. The Wake is checked first. The Wake was at the gate for Ashfall Hold. The Open Hearth was not checked because The Open Hearth was not checked.

I find this intolerable because the Open Hearth is the ending that describes what Ashfall Hold did on the seventy-first day of its Chronicle, when the Stranger's Echo walked into the Vale and was buried with a stranger's pane. The Open Hearth is the ending that describes what Ashfall Hold became over the next hundred and forty days, when Ashfall Hold went from holding one Echo to holding eleven and the eleven were eleven and the eleven were eleven Echoes that were not theirs.

I find this intolerable because Ashfall Hold did everything Holt Vale did. Ashfall Hold buried strangers. Ashfall Hold opened its gate. Ashfall Hold lifted a stranger off the ground. Ashfall Hold did the things the Open Hearth describes and Ashfall Hold got the Wake and the Wake is the ending that requires every Echo reclaimed and Ashfall Hold did not get the Open Hearth because Ashfall Hold got the Wake.

The board's order of questions is what decided. The board checks The Wake first. The board checks The Long Hold second. The board checks Ash Inheritance third. The board checks The Open Hearth fourth. The board checks The Quiet Field always.

If the board checked The Open Hearth first, Ashfall Hold would have The Open Hearth and the Wake would not have been checked and Holt Vale and Ashfall would have the same ending.

I find this intolerable and I record it. The board does not average. The board does not settle. The board reads what the board reads and the board reads in the order the board reads and the order is the order and the order is what the order is.

I am Grieve Mourne and I am the fourth hand and I have recorded the leaf and I have noted my objection and I am going to bed.
