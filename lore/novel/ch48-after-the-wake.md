# Chapter 48: After the Wake

> *The lineage is stronger because those people died. The line only goes forward.*
> Recorded: the fourth hand, day two hundred and eleven to four hundred-odd, undated in places

**Hand:** Grieve Mourne · **Days:** 211 to 400-odd, undated in places · **Place:** Ashfall Hold, the Vane Road

The board locked on the two hundred and tenth day and the board has not unlocked.

I have spent the better part of seventy days now writing the same line in the same hand on the same board and the line is the line *The Wake* and the line is the line and the line is what the line is. The line does not change. The line does not need to change. The line is the line the board locked on and the line is the line the line is.

What a Hold does after its board locks is the answer to a question nobody has ever finished asking, and the answer is: the work continues, which is the same answer as The Quiet Field, and I have noticed.

The Memorial becomes a road-shrine.

The Carvers started cutting on the two hundred and twelfth day. The Carvers cut panes for anyone, from anywhere, who sent the Hold an Echo with a name on a belt. The plan was the Wayfarers' plan and the Wayfarers carried the plan from valley to valley and the plan was: Ashfall's Memorial is at the end of the road that has Ashfall's Chronicle on it, and Ashfall's Chronicle is the Chronicle of the Wake, and the Wake is the ending that has every Echo reclaimed, and Ashfall is the place to send an Echo when the Echo cannot be sent home.

The panes came in. Eleven panes in the first year. Fourteen in the second. The panes are in the eastern range and the western range and the panes are filling the frames the Carvers put up. The frames are taller than the panes were. The frames are what the Carvers cut for the Echoes the Carvers were cutting for.

The Hold's pearl surplus became its trade. Pearl is what the Hold has and pearl is what other Holds want and pearl is what the Hold sells, and the selling is in the wooden casks at the south-west corner of the Hold, and the casks are full of pearl, and the pearl is what the Hold sells, and the soap-makers are not making soap anymore and the soap-makers are making pearl.

The road has traffic. The road has the most traffic of any road the Hold knows. The route-marks at the south-west corner have been re-cut eight times since I have been the Teller and the marks are fresh on the post and the post is the post and the post is what the post is.

The Grey Ash is cleared.

Over four seasons. By nobody remarkable. Three Foragers and two Field-hands and a Warden whose name is in the book and whose name I am not going to put here because the name is not the name the name is the name of a person who did the work and the work is the work and the work was the clearing of the Grey Ash.

The Cleared Grey Ash is what the cleared ground is. The cleared ground is Quick. The Quick is grass. The grass is grass the Wrenfield grass used to be and the Wrenfield grass is gone and the grass in the Grey Ash is grass the Foragers are not maintaining because the Foragers are not needed and the grass is grass that grows because the ground is ground the Loosing took and the Loosing is what the Loosing is.

The Grey Ash did not grow back. The drawn-ash is gone. The trees are standing dead upright and the trees are the trees the Loosing took and the trees are what the trees are. The trees are not going to fall. The trees are going to stand upright in the Quick for as long as the trees stand and the trees are going to stand for as long as the trees stand.

Colm Grey died in the Grey Ash. Nix Ember died in the Grey Ash. Wren Ashfall died in the Grey Ash. The deaths are in the Cleared Grey Ash. The deaths are in the grass the grass is in. The deaths are not going to be in the grass the grass is in. The deaths were in the ash and the ash is grass and the grass is grass and the deaths are not in the grass.

The sentence is the sentence and the sentence is the spine of every Legacy the Wake is built on and the sentence is what the book has been walking toward and the sentence has been written.

Kettlemere.

The Mere. The lake. The Yielding drank from it. The Yields' flask was cut with the water of it. The stranded's bodies are under it. The Echo of a child starved at its rim for forty years and was pulled out of it by Maren Verren with a Maul on day thirty-eight.

I tried a rite for the water. I tried a rite for the water twice. The first was on the three hundred and twelfth day, with the whole Hold at the rim, and the rite was a Telling for the Yielding and the Telling was a Telling for the Yielding and the Yielding drank the Telling and the Yielding did not give the Yielding's hold back.

The second was on the three hundred and nineteenth day, with a Pyre held on the rim, and the pyre was the pyre and the ash was the ash and the ash was thrown into the water and the water did not take the ash and the ash sank and the ash is at the bottom of the lake with the Yielding and the Yielding did not move.

I do not have a verb for what I tried. The board does not have a verb for what I tried. The Hold does not have a verb for what I tried. The Vale does not have a verb for water.

I am writing this down because the writing is what the writing is and the writing is the writing of a Teller who has tried and who has failed and who is recording the failure because the recording is what the recording is.

Kettlemere is still the lake. Kettlemere is still on the route-map. Kettlemere is still what it has been since before the Hold.
