**Hand:** Grieve Mourne, fourth hand, first entries · **Days:** 148 to 156 · **Place:** Wrenfield, the Hold

Wrenfield went Gone on the one hundred and fifty-second day.

·  ·  ·

Wrenfield is the field the Hold eats from. Wrenfield is the field the Hold has eaten from since the founding. Wrenfield is the field that was pushed back from Greying on the forty-seventh day in an eleven-day piece of work that cost twenty-two people and four numbers on four axes that were the best single entry the Hold has ever written. Wrenfield is the field that has been Quick and Turning and Greying and Quick again and Greying again and Greying and Greying and Greying for the season and a half before the one hundred and fifty-second day.

Wrenfield went Gone because of three things and not one thing.

The first thing is three seasons of nobody maintaining. The maintenance of Wrenfield is the Carvers and the Foragers and the Foragers have not been maintaining because the Foragers have been at the kiln and at the panes and at the Memorial and at the Smithy. The Carvers have been at the long grass and at the Smithy. The maintenance is what holds Wrenfield against drawn-ash and the maintenance has not been holding because the maintenance has not been done and the drawn-ash has been going around the maintenance and the drawn-ash has been feeding Wrenfield and Wrenfield has been going Greying.

The second thing is a roaming Echo's crossings. Nix Ember's Echo had been roaming since the one hundred and seventy-six day, and the Echo was in the Wrenfield long grass on the one hundred and forty-ninth day and the one hundred and fiftieth day and the one hundred and fifty-first day, and the Echo was feeding on Wrenfield and Wrenfield was going Greying and the Echo was going toward Greying and the Echo was the Echo.

The third thing is the palisade. The palisade was tested on the one hundred and thirty-fourth day and the palisade held and the Hold's labour went to the palisade after the palisade. The labour went to the palisade instead of the furrows. The furrows were Wrenfield's furrows. Wrenfield's furrows were the furrows the Wrenfield Monument is holding at eleven paces of Quick in the middle of Gone ground and the Monument is holding the eleven paces because the relic under the Monument is doing the work the relic was bound to do.

Three things. Not one thing. Wrenfield went Gone on the one hundred and fifty-second day because the three things happened at the same time and the same time was the time the three things happened.

The board read what the board reads when a region goes Gone. Minus four on Memory. Minus seven on Mercy. Minus five on Communion. Minus four on Dominion.

Gone is what Wrenfield is and Gone is what Wrenfield is for. Wrenfield is Gone the way a Hold that has stopped going out is Gone. Wrenfield is Gone in the body the way Gone is gone. Wrenfield is the field the Hold eats from and Wrenfield is the field the Hold cannot eat from.

The Cull are proved right.
