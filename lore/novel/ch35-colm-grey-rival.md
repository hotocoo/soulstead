# Chapter 35: Colm Grey, Rival

> *Never liked them. Will still carry the blade.*
> Recorded: the third hand, day eighty-four to eighty-five, with the reign to one hundred and nine summarised forward

**Hand:** Asha Vane · **Days:** 84 to 85, with the reign to 109 summarised forward · **Place:** Stonewake Scarp, the Hold

Maren Verren fell on the eighty-fourth day, in the same scarp, to the same kind of thing, at eighteen of damage and a reach of thirty she had stopped measuring, and the place she died in is fifty feet from where Rin Ashfall died in another reign and sixty feet from where she will be reclaimed on the ninety-first day, and the scarp has now taken two wielders in the same fifty feet and the Hold still sends people there because the lime is there and the lime is what the Smithy is for and the Smithy is what the Hold is for.

Her reign is sixty-nine days. Sixty-nine world-days, which is sixty-nine days of the world's own counting and most of a working life. She is the longest wielder the Hold has had and the longest wielder the Hold is going to have.

She said something about the quarry face. I am going to write it down because the Hold is going to need it for the rest of the book. She said the quarry face was where the stone got cut, and the stone got cut where it got cut because the stone was the stone, and the stone was not going to get cut where it did not get cut, and the cutting was the cutting and the cutting was the cutting.

The Echo went down at the bottom of the scarp with her Maul still in its hand and the Maul's bond-memory in its plate and the bond-memory in its plate is the relic that is going to be the relic the next wielder is going to spend on something. The Echo tiered to the third by the night of the eighty-seventh. The Echo was roaming by the morning of the eighty-ninth.

The board read what the board read. Plus two on Memory. Zero on Mercy. Zero on Communion. Minus three on Dominion.

The wake was not held. The wake was a word the Hold did not yet have a use for. The wake is a word the Hold is going to have a use for in a hundred and twenty-six days.

The Succession was held that evening and three names were called because three names are always called. The three names were Colm Grey, who had disliked Maren for eleven years about a fence. Yeva Holt, who was needed in the fields and was not going to be called from the fields. Sable Kettle, who was nineteen and cooking.

The three names were the three names the Hold had. Two were declined. The first to be called was Sable Kettle, who declined because she was cooking. The second was Yeva Holt, who declined because the fields needed her and the Hold could not lose a Field-hand. The third was Colm Grey, who did not decline because Colm Grey had been waiting for the call for eleven years.

I am going to write what the call said, and the call said what calls say. *Never liked them. Will still carry the blade.*

The rival's arithmetic is the arithmetic of a man who has decided never to be close to anything again. Ten in a hundred more Composure to spend, which is the most Composure a wielder can start with. A tenth less Resolve. And her Echo's pull does not touch him at all, which is the only reason the Hold chose him and the only reason the Hold can defend the choice.

The rival's arithmetic is also the arithmetic of a man who cannot grieve. A rival does not grieve. A rival has never grieved. A rival has the grip of a man who never opened his grip to the person he did not like and the grip is closed because the grip has never been opened, and the grip is the purse and the purse is full.

The Purse is full. The grip is closed. The rival walks out into the field and the Echo of the woman he did not like is standing in the grass and the Echo's pull does not touch him and the Echo is two bodies' worth of skill and the Echo is at the rim of the long grass and the Echo is going to be his for a season.

He took the Reaper Pike off the wall. The Pike is on the wall with a broken head. The head had been broken in the same scarp, by the same Gravewarden, in the same fight, in the same hour. The head had not survived the putting down. The head was on the Smithy floor.

I argued with him about the head. The argument is in another chapter and I am not going to repeat it here. The argument is about whether to bind a relic into the head or to spend the relic on stone, and the argument is two days long and the argument is what I am writing this chapter for and not what the chapter is about. The chapter is about Colm Grey.

He took the Pike with the stub. He took the Pike out into the field. He put down Maren's Echo with the Pike's stub on the ninety-first day, and the stub broke in the Echo and he finished it with his bare hands, and the Echo went down and the relic came out and the relic was Maren's and the relic was spent on stone in Brackwood by an argument the chapter is not repeating.

He took the Pike back to the wall. He took the Pike off the wall when the Pike had a new head. The head had been bound with a relic and the relic had been somebody and the somebody is not in this chapter. The Pike remembered a long swing for cutting grass at the edge of the long grass, which is what the original Pike remembered, which is what the Field-hand's tool remembered, which is what the relic in the new head was bound for.

The Pike's reach was forty. The Pike's arc was one body. The Pike was a Pike.

The reign to come was the reign the Hold remembers least well and the reign the Hold understands best.

He did not go into Wrenfield. Not once. The reign was a reign of the scarp and the scarp only, and the scarp was the Gravewardens' ground and the Gravewardens' ground was where the relic was and the relic was what the Pike was bound with and the Pike was the weapon and the weapon was the scarp.

He went out at first light. He came back at dusk. He put down a Gravewarden on the eighty-eighth day and a Gravewarden on the ninety-fourth day and an Ash Husk in the Grey Ash on the ninety-ninth day and a Gravewarden on the one hundred and third day. He never went into the Grey Ash on a day that was not the day of an Echo he had been told was at the Ash Orchard. He went to the Ash Orchard on the one hundred and third day because the Echo at the Ash Orchard was the Echo of a person the Hold had lost and the Echo was at the fifth growth and the palisade was the palisade.

The Echo at the Ash Orchard was not at the fifth growth. The Echo at the Ash Orchard was at the fourth. The Echo at the Ash Orchard was a Gravewarden that had walked out of a barrow and the Echo at the Ash Orchard said the name of a person the Hold had never heard of. Colm Grey killed it on the one hundred and third day. He took the relic. He did not bind it into the Pike because the Pike was bound and Ketil Brack had said one relic per blade and the saying was the saying.

The relic went on the Smithy shelf. The relic is on the shelf as I write this. The relic is going to be on the shelf for a long time.

He died on the one hundred and ninth day, in the Grey Ash, in the same ground, to an Ash Husk, at nine of damage, because he was out of everything. He was out of everything because he had gone out on a day that was not the day of an Echo he had been told was at the Ash Orchard, because an Echo had been at the Ash Orchard and the Echo was not at the Ash Orchard any more and the Echo had walked into the Grey Ash and the Echo had walked into the ground Colm Grey was in and the Echo had killed him with nine of damage because he was out of everything.

The Echo was not his Echo. The Echo was not anybody's Echo the Hold could name. The Echo was an Echo that had walked out of a valley nobody could identify, and the Echo was carrying the build it had died with, and the build was at the level the Echo had been fed to, and the Echo was at the three growth, and the Echo was roaming, and the Echo was in the Grey Ash, and the Echo killed a wielder of Ashfall Hold on the one hundred and ninth day because the wielder was out of everything.

The wake was held. The wake was the wake the Hold had not yet learned to call a wake. The wake was a Telling and a Pyre and a Telling held for the dead of the one hundred and ninth day. The Telling was the Telling the Hold has been holding since the twenty-first day. The Pyre was the second Pyre of the Hold. The Pyre raised the ration by one and the ration became four.

The board moved. The board read what the board reads when a wielder dies. Plus two on Memory. Zero on Mercy. Zero on Communion. Minus three on Dominion.

The Succession was held. Three names were called.

The first was Sable Kettle, who did not decline.

The Pike went on the wall. The Pike came down from the wall when Sable Kettle took the Pike off the wall and put the Pike back on the wall because Sable Kettle was not going to carry a Pike. Sable Kettle carried the Hearth Knife. The Hearth Knife is in another chapter.

This chapter is about Colm Grey.

Colm Grey never liked Maren Verren. Colm Grey carried the blade for twenty-five days. Colm Grey died on the one hundred and ninth day with nothing in his grip and an Ash Husk at his feet and the scarp behind him and the Hold behind the scarp and the Pike on his back and the Pike's binding on the Pike and the Pike's binding the motion of a man cutting grass at the edge of long grass, which is what the relic was bound for, which is what the smith who bound it was for, which is what the man was for.

The Pike is on the wall. The Pike has a head. The Pike has a binding.

The man is in the Grey Ash. The man is the man.
